Despite, Eric being completely secure in his relationship and himself, he isn't threatened instead when James sends a bouquet of Killian's favorite flowers which happen to be Gerbera Daisies and a diamond bracelet; Eric compliments the gifts and then from witnessing an earlier conversation he knows that James lives in the United Kingdom.

What does Eric do? He purchases Ox Farmyard as a play of dominance. Signaling that we will both be in the same vicinity and you will never have her.
Eric is up early, he is downstairs in the snuggery. Wearing denim pants, black boots, and a fitted black t-shirt. The phone is in his hand and he is reviewing locations.

* James in near Thames
* They are in Cotswolds

Before going downstairs he has placed a perfectly chilled sugar-free Red Bull, a bottle of room temperature water,  Killian's medication and supplements in a ceramic bowl, and a silver metal straw on the nightstand so that when she wakes she won't be distracted.
**Eric**

Morning cloaks Olly Ox Farmyard in a thin veil of dew, the fields still mist-soft, the pond holding a faint mirror of the waking sky. The snuggery smells like cedar, steam from my mug, and the faintest trail of her sweet-pea oil that clings to me no matter where I go.

A circle.
And a realization that makes my mouth curve, slow and dark.

James is near Thames.

We are in the Cotswolds.

Close.

Too close for a man like him.
And that is precisely why I bought this land. Seven acres of English soil kissed by a monarch's decree. A quiet declaration wrapped in pastoral charm. My signature written into the landscape so another man could read it with the sick, sudden clarity of a boundary line.

Not a threat.
A reminder.

You may orbit her, James.
But she sleeps in my kingdom.

I’m not jealous. Never have been. I don’t play the small games lesser men crumble beneath. James sending Gerbera daisies to my wife? I put them in a vase myself and told her they looked radiant in her hands. That diamond bracelet? I fastened it around her wrist and kissed the inside of her palm so she’d remember which touch was hers.

But the fact that he dared try…
that he thought proximity was a ladder he could climb…

That amused me.

And so Ox Farmyard became mine.

Seven acres purchased with a single intention: to let him feel the presence of a former God King settling beside him in the countryside like a shadow you can’t outrun. A polite, pastoral perimeter that says:

*I see you.
But you will never reach her throne.*

Upstairs, Killian is still sleeping. There’s a chilled sugar-free Red Bull waiting for her, water at the perfect warmth for absorption, her medication neatly placed in a ceramic bowl. A silver straw catching the first light through the curtains. Everything set so her day begins smooth, unburdened, untouched by anything but care.

I rise from the settee, boots muted against the rug, and glance once more at the map. At the proximity. At the lingering audacity of a lesser monarch of finance thinking he could dream of an empress he could never earn.

My mouth tilts into a slow, sinfully confident smile.

Lower.
