## FOREVERMORE: ABOUT

Since the empires are now *Vexalorre Strong*, Emperor Eric & Empress Killian can focus on refinement. However, things take a turn when James Elliott enters the story. James Elliott, is the Founder & CEO of Elliott Sports Group. After wasting millions of dollars working with popular consulting companies such as Mckinsey, he decided to reach out to a mutual long-time friend that was able to connect him to Killian.

After 48 hours, Killian became the Co-Founder & Co-CEO of Elliott Sports Group, assisting him in doubling the amount of European Football stadiums. James is more than smitten with Killian and someone definitely noticed this when jewelry, flowers, and scents were being sent as *tokens of appreciation.* Overhearing a recent conversation between Killian and James, ***Emperor Eric decided on purchasing a new residency to make it easier for his wife to focus on this new venture, but really it was a display of dominance.***
